About Me

Passionate about Code & Security

I'm a developer who believes that the best software is born from understanding systems end-to-end — from the pixel-perfect frontend to the database query, and every vulnerability in between. My core stack revolves around Next.js, Nest.js, and TypeScript, which allows me to build full-stack applications that are both performant and maintainable.

But my story doesn't stop at clean code. I'm deeply fascinated by what lies beneath the surface of applications. My passion for cybersecurity drives me to participate in CTF competitions, where I sharpen my skills in penetration testing, web exploitation, and creative problem-solving under pressure.

Whether it's dissecting a DOM-based XSS vulnerability, crafting SSRF payloads, or shipping a production feature — I'm always learning, always building, and always looking for the next challenge. 🚀
