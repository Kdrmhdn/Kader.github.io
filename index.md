## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a developer who believes that the best software is born from understanding systems end-to-end — from the pixel-perfect frontend to the database query, and every vulnerability in between. My core stack revolves around **Next.js**, **Nest.js**, and **TypeScript**, which allows me to build full-stack applications that are both performant and maintainable.

But my story doesn't end at clean code. I'm deeply fascinated by what lies *beneath the surface* of applications. My passion for **cybersecurity** drives me to participate in **CTF (Capture The Flag) competitions**, where I sharpen my skills in penetration testing, web exploitation, and problem-solving under pressure. Whether it's dissecting a DOM-based XSS vulnerability or crafting SSRF payloads, I love the thrill of the hunt.

I'm always learning, always building, and always looking for the next challenge — be it shipping a feature or capturing a flag. 🚀
